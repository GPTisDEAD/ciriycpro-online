# Авито «Грузчики» — фото 4: упаковка техники

**Куда:** четвёртая карточка
**Формат:** 4:3

## Промпт для Gemini (новая сессия)

```
Documentary industrial photo, 35mm realistic style.
Modern Moscow apartment living room during move-out: hands in orange
work gloves wrapping a large flat-screen TV with a layer of bubble
wrap, then a layer of cling stretch film. The TV stands against a
wall with other wrapped electronics beside it. A roll of stretch
film and tape dispenser visible on the floor. Natural daylight.
Industrial realistic, graphite + warm orange accents, shallow depth
of field on the wrapping hands.

Aspect: 4:3. Photorealistic.

Negative: no faces, no body above wrists, no visible brand logos
on the TV or boxes, no text overlays, no watermark, no cartoon,
no 3D render, no cgi,
single scene, no composite, no collage, no split frame.
```

## Проверка
- Руки оранжевые в перчатках
- Телевизор в плёнке
- Плёнка-стрейч, скотч
- Крупный план деталей
