**Куда:** третья карточка
**Формат:** 4:3

## Промпт для Gemini (новая сессия)

```
Documentary industrial photo, 35mm realistic style.
Open-air site next to a Moscow residential building: a large yellow-
orange 8m³ construction debris dumpster container, filled halfway
with broken concrete and plasterboard. Three workers in orange
hi-vis vests, seen from behind, forming a short chain tossing more
debris bags into the container. Dust catches afternoon light. A
sledgehammer leans against the container as a prop. Industrial
realistic, graphite + warm orange palette.

Aspect: 4:3. Photorealistic.

Negative: no faces, no corporate logos on vests or container,
no text, no watermark, no cartoon, no 3D render, no cgi,
single scene, no composite, no collage, no split frame.
```

- Жёлто-оранжевый контейнер 8м³
