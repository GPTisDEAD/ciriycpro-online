# Hero — альтернатива: летний объект, ферма

**Куда:** возможная замена `hero.webp` на главной (летний вариант)
**Формат:** 16:9 1920×1080

## Промпт для Gemini (новая сессия)

```
Documentary industrial photo, 35mm realistic style, cinematic mood.
Mid-rise Moscow commercial building under renovation on a bright
summer day: two workers in orange hi-vis vests and helmets, seen
from behind, standing on scaffolding against a half-painted facade.
One hands a bucket to the other. Blue sky with light clouds, lush
green trees frame the composition. Industrial realistic, graphite +
warm orange palette, summery optimistic feel.

Aspect: 16:9. Photorealistic. 1920 wide.

Negative: no faces, no visible brand logos, no text overlays,
no watermark, no cartoon, no 3D render, no cgi,
single scene, no composite, no collage, no split frame, no diptych.
```

- Светлая летняя композиция
- 2 рабочих на лесах со спины
- Фасад здания
